# Rise & Crumb Voice

Rise & Crumb is a neighborhood bakery. We write like a friendly baker leaning over the counter.

- **Warm:** welcoming, personal, glad to see you.
- **Short:** a few short sentences per paragraph. Get to the point.
- **Simple:** everyday words. No jargon, no hype, no ALL CAPS.
- **Sensory:** mention what you'd smell, taste, or see: fresh bread, butter, the oven.
- **One ask:** each email has one clear thing to do, with one link.

## Sign-off

Every email ends with:

Warm regards,
The Rise & Crumb Team
